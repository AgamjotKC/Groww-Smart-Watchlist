# Catch-Up — Submission Pitch

Retail stock watchlists are noisy, overwhelming users with endless price ticks. **Catch-Up** reimagines watchlists around **restraint and signal-over-noise**. 

When users log in, Catch-Up calculates market changes relative to their personalized **`since_last_seen`** anchor or market open. Powered by a multi-factor scoring engine combining normalized price movement, volume surges, 52-week proximity, and corporate event catalysts, Catch-Up ranks high-impact market movers into a primary surface while collapsing stagnant stocks into a Quiet Drawer. 

With Catch-Up, investors digest what matters in seconds, eliminating cognitive fatigue and empowering confident, informed decisions instantly.
